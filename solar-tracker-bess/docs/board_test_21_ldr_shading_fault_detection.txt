Goal
----

Use the real locked LDR tracker and panel-power stream to detect panel shading,
publish the warning through the existing communication bridge, display it on
the dashboard, and prove automatic recovery after the obstruction is removed.

Detection Rules
---------------

```text
Sensor near zero or ADC rail:
  Hold both servo axes and publish Sensor Fault / Sensor Saturation.

Combined LDR signal below the configured minimum:
  Hold both servo axes and publish Low Light.

Panel shading:
  Learn panel power per total LDR count only while tracker.state=locked.
  Warn after three consecutive samples below 50% of the learned baseline.
  Clear only after three consecutive recovered samples.
```

Live Procedure
--------------

```text
1. Keep the LDR cross and panel unobstructed while the panel converges.
2. Dashboard Protection first shows Standby, then Learning after lock.
3. Do not cover the panel until Protection changes from Learning to OK.
4. Leave all four LDRs exposed; cover only the solar-panel face for at least
   three packet intervals (about two seconds).
5. Confirm dashboard Protection changes to Shading.
6. Uncover the panel and leave everything stationary.
7. Confirm Protection returns to OK after at least three recovered packets.
8. Let the run finish or stop it after recovery evidence is logged.
```

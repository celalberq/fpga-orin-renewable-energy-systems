Goal
----

Test two MG90S servos through the PCA9685 before mounting the solar panel:

```text
Orin Nano Super -> I2C -> PCA9685 -> pan servo on channel 15
                                  -> tilt servo on channel 12
```

This proves the actuator stage needed for AI-assisted solar tracking.

Wiring
------

Keep the PCA9685 wiring from Board Test 09:

```text
Orin pin 1  3.3V -> PCA9685 VCC
Orin pin 6  GND  -> PCA9685 GND
Orin pin 3  SDA  -> PCA9685 SDA
Orin pin 5  SCL  -> PCA9685 SCL

5V adapter + -> PCA9685 V+
5V adapter - -> PCA9685 GND
```

Servo channels:

```text
Pan servo  -> channel 15
Tilt servo -> channel 12
```

Servo wire orientation:

```text
Brown/black   -> GND row
Red           -> V+ row
Orange/yellow -> PWM signal row
```

First command
-------------

Center both servos:

```text
cd /home/msi
python3 solar-tracker-bess/code/orin_app/pan_tilt_servo_test.py --bus 7 --addr 0x40 --pan-channel 15 --tilt-channel 12 --pan-min 70 --pan-max 110 --tilt-min 80 --tilt-max 100 --mode center
```

Safety limits
-------------

```text
Do not mount the solar panel hard until both servos move correctly.
If the bracket hits a mechanical stop, unplug the 5V servo adapter.
If the servo buzzes hard, reduce the angle range.
Current safe bench range is 70-110 degrees pan and 80-100 degrees tilt.
```

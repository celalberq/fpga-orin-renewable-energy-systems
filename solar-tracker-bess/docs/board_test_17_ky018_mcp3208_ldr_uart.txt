Goal
----

Read the solar-panel divider and four KY-018 light sensors through one MCP3208.
This is a sensor bring-up test; the panel may remain physically separate from
the pan-tilt mechanism.

Verified electrical checks
--------------------------

```text
MCP3208 VDD pin 16: 3.3V
MCP3208 VREF pin 15: 3.3V
Panel divider midpoint: 0.3V during the check
All four KY-018 signals: about 0.9V ambient, up to 3.3V with flashlight
```

ADC channel map
---------------

```text
CH0 / pin 1: panel 100k/22k divider midpoint
CH1 / pin 2: GND
CH2 / pin 3: top-left KY-018 signal
CH3 / pin 4: top-right KY-018 signal
CH4 / pin 5: bottom-left KY-018 signal
CH5 / pin 6: bottom-right KY-018 signal
```

Expected line
-------------

```text
ldr,seq=00001,pv=01665,tl=1117,tr=1200,bl=1300,br=1400
```

Pass conditions
---------------

```text
1. A valid line arrives every 0.5 seconds.
2. Panel voltage follows the CH0 divider measurement.
3. Flashing or covering one KY-018 changes only its matching labeled count.
4. Every LDR count remains between 0 and 4095.
5. LED7 warns that at least one LDR is close to ADC full scale.
```

Centered calibration run
------------------------

```text
Date: 2026-08-08
Packets: 01280 through 01303, 24 consecutive samples
Raw data: solar-tracker-bess/data/ldr_center_calibration_2026_08_08.csv

Mean TL: 2892.54 counts
Mean TR: 2835.63 counts
Mean BL: 3121.79 counts
Mean BR: 3257.29 counts

Mean normalized horizontal error, (right-left)/total: +0.00636
Mean normalized vertical error, (bottom-top)/total: +0.05414
Mean right/left ratio: 1.01283
Mean bottom/top ratio: 1.11471
```

The closed-loop controller should subtract the normalized center errors before
applying its deadband. This prevents sensor gain and mounting offsets from
causing motion when the lamp is centered.

Sensor-left calibration run
---------------------------

```text
Date: 2026-08-08
Packets: 01603 through 01622, 20 consecutive samples
Raw data: solar-tracker-bess/data/ldr_left_calibration_2026_08_08.csv

Mean left sum: 6613.90 counts
Mean right sum: 6365.60 counts
Mean raw normalized horizontal error: -0.01922
Mean center-corrected horizontal error: -0.02558
Center-corrected horizontal range: about -0.0292 through -0.0146
```

The sensor-left sign is therefore negative when horizontal error is defined as
`(right-left)/total`. The left run remains outside a 0.01 normalized deadband,
while the centered run remains inside it.

was small. Startup center calibration is therefore preferable to permanently
hard-coding one earlier center offset.

LED map
-------

```text
LED0 ADC transaction activity
LED1 half-second heartbeat
LED2 UART activity
LED3 right sum greater than left sum
LED4 left sum greater than right sum
LED5 bottom sum greater than top sum
LED6 top sum greater than bottom sum
LED7 at least one LDR count above 4000
```

Notes
-----

```text
Live data confirmed that KY-018 counts increase with stronger light. The four
modules still need a uniform-light calibration before raw left/right differences
drive a servo. A detached panel can verify voltage sensing, but it cannot
demonstrate that pan motion improves panel power.
```

Hardware Decision: solar-tracker-bess Solar
===============================

Current decision:

Buy the low-voltage sensing/protection parts first. Do not buy solar panel, battery, or full buck converter hardware yet.

Needed now:

```text
1. Nexys Video
2. Laptop/PC
3. USB programming cable, included with Nexys Video kit
4. Nexys Video 12V supply, included with kit
5. Breadboard
6. Jumper wires
7. Multimeter
```

Buy for the first real sensing stage:

```text
1. MCP3208 DIP-16 ADC
   Use: SPI ADC for voltage/current sensing.
   Decision: buy now.
   First use:
     CH0 = voltage divider
     CH1 = analog current-sensor signal after calibration

2. INA226 IIC current/power monitor module
   Use: voltage/current/power telemetry and calibration.
   Decision: buy now.
   First use:
     calibration/reference path
   Later use:
     FPGA or Orin I2C current/power sensing

3. Logic level converter 3.3V-5V
   Use: only for modules that are not 3.3V compatible.
   Decision: buy because it is cheap and useful.

4. Resistor set
   Use: voltage divider and test loads.
   Decision: buy.

5. Fuses
   Use: input protection before any power experiments.
   Decision: buy 1A, 2A, 5A.
```

Buy after simulation and PWM logic are ready:

```text
1. Low-voltage DC supply or small 12V source with current limit.
2. Proper MOSFET gate driver.
3. MOSFET.
4. Inductor, diode, capacitors, and shunt resistor for buck converter.
5. Power resistor load.
6. Small solar panel or PV emulator.
```

Do not buy yet:

```text
1. Battery pack
2. High-power solar panel
3. Grid inverter
4. Large electronic load
5. High-voltage components
```

Battery decision update:

```text
Battery is allowed later only as a measured storage element, not as a supply for
Nexys Video or Orin.

Safe later buy:
  protected 1S LiPo/Li-ion battery
  proper 1S charger/protection module
  small safe load

First battery use:
  measure voltage/current/state
  report SoC and charge/discharge state
  keep Nexys Video and Orin on their official adapters
```

Why this is enough:

```text
The first solar version should prove MPPT logic, PWM timing, sensor reading, and telemetry before real power conversion.
This lowers risk and keeps the Nexys Video safe.
```

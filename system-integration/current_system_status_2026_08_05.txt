renewable-energy tracker telemetry system.


Current mechanical limitation
-----------------------------

The new pan/tilt servo mechanism works electrically and moves under Orin
tracker control. The solar panel is now mounted on a cardboard adapter/base and
stabilized with 4-5 elastic bands, which is acceptable for prototype evidence.
The remaining mechanical polish is to fix the base to a heavier board, keep the
panel balanced, leave wire slack near the tilt hinge, and collect stronger
light/sun data.

2026-08-07 update:

The tilt part has now broken, so old servo/camera live-pass evidence is still
valid but the current physical tilt axis should be treated as unsafe for new
two-axis servo demo runs. Use pan-only or software-only tracker demos while the
mechanism is repaired. The current repair recommendation is documented in:

```text
solar-tracker-bess/docs/servo_mechanics_repair_research_2026_08_07.txt
```

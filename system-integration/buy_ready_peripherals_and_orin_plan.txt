Do not buy yet
--------------

```text
1. Large solar panel.
2. Battery pack.
3. Grid-tied inverter.
4. High-voltage power electronics.
5. SDR/RF hardware.
6. FMC ADC/DAC board.
7. Large electronic load.
8. Oscilloscope, unless budget allows or you cannot borrow one.
```

Checkout checklist
------------------

Before ordering:

```text
1. Recheck current price and stock on the day of purchase.
2. Confirm every sensor module can run at 3.3V or has safe level shifting.
3. Confirm Orin listing is the Super Developer Kit, not only a module.
4. Confirm Orin accessories included by the seller.
5. Keep invoice and product links.
6. Prefer Turkey sellers for cheap peripherals.
7. Use Germany/EU fallback for Orin only if Turkey stock/price is bad.
```

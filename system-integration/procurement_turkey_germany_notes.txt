Procurement Notes: Turkey and Germany
=====================================

Problem:

Digilent distributors in Turkey can be out of stock for long periods. A Germany/EU purchase route is realistic if a family member can receive or carry the board.

Preferred buying routes:

```text
Route A: Try direct Digilent-to-Turkey first.
  1. Add the exact board to Digilent cart.
  2. Enter Turkey shipping address.
  3. Check whether the checkout accepts the order.
  4. Expect Digilent to request extra end-use/export information for Turkey.
  5. Confirm total shipping cost and import handling risk before paying.

Route B: Germany fallback.
  1. Buy from Digilent or a reliable EU/Germany seller.
  2. Have the board delivered to Germany.
  3. Sister brings one board to Turkey as personal luggage for personal study/project use.
  4. Keep the invoice, order confirmation, and product page/specification.
  5. Recheck Turkey passenger customs limits before the flight.
  6. Do not buy many identical boards because that can look commercial.
```

Digilent direct-shipping note:

Digilent's shipping page does not list Turkey as a country where direct shipping is impossible. It says Turkey orders require extra EUS/EUI information before shipping, and Turkey is included in Digilent's EU Region chart for product availability.

Source:

```text
https://digilent.com/shop/shipping-returns/
```

Important customs note:

This is not legal advice. Rules and value limits can change. Before buying, check the current Turkey customs/Ticaret Bakanligi passenger goods rules. A Germany/EU purchase does not automatically avoid Turkish customs rules when the item enters Turkey.

Germany VAT note:

Germany prices normally include VAT. VAT refund/tax-free shopping may be possible only under the correct conditions, usually for non-EU residents exporting goods in personal luggage with proper paperwork. If your sister lives in Germany/EU and buys it as a German/EU resident, do not assume a tourist VAT refund applies.

Warranty note:

If the board is bought in Germany or from an EU seller, warranty/RMA will likely need to go back through that seller. Keep packaging and invoice.

Transport note:

```text
1. Carry the FPGA board in original box or anti-static bag.
2. Keep it in hand luggage if possible.
3. Remove nothing from the board.
4. Keep accessories, USB cable, and invoice together.
5. FPGA boards normally do not contain lithium batteries, so airline battery rules are usually not the issue.
```

Board priority if using Germany/EU route:

```text
Best technical fit:
  Nexys Video, if available and affordable.

Best network-focused fit:
  Zybo Z7-20, because it has Gigabit Ethernet.

Best practical pure-FPGA fit:
  Arty A7-100T, because it is balanced, documented, and lower risk.

Avoid as main shared board:
  Basys 3, because it lacks Ethernet.
```

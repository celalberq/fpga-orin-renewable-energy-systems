Priority identity used for school selection
-------------------------------------------

Use the same priority stack as the project portfolio:

```text
Main strength:
  Computer engineering + renewable energy systems

Strong support:
  Digital communication, signal processing, telemetry, and FPGA data movement

Basic support:
  Networking, Ethernet literacy, SCADA/infrastructure monitoring awareness
```

This means the best master's program is not pure power engineering, pure telecom, or pure networking.
The best program is electrical/computer engineering that allows renewable energy, power systems, control,
embedded systems, telemetry, data, or communication electives/projects.

Application target shape
------------------------

Use this target mix:

```text
1. Two Melbourne-first electrical/computer/renewable programs
2. One practical Melbourne backup
3. One technical stretch outside Melbourne
4. One energy-systems alternative only if the career target shifts more toward energy planning/policy
```

Tier 1: Best fit / first application targets
--------------------------------------------

1. Monash University

```text
Target program:
  Master of Professional Engineering, Electrical Engineering specialisation
  Course code: E6011
  Qualification name: Master of Professional Electrical Engineering

Why it fits:
  - Strong Melbourne option.
  - Fully professional engineering pathway.
  - Electrical engineering can support renewables, power, embedded/FPGA, controls, and telemetry.
  - Good brand for Australian applications and graduate employability.
  - Best if the goal is a broad professional engineering path with renewable/embedded focus.

How to present our portfolio:
  - Lead with solar-tracker-bess solar/BESS/SCADA-style renewable monitoring.
  - Use Nexys Video FPGA work as the computer engineering differentiator.
  - Use fpga-digital-communications to show signal-processing and telemetry strength.
  - Keep network-telemetry-dashboard as basic Ethernet/infrastructure support.

Risk:
  - Entry requirement references a high credit / 65% average.
  - Your GPA around 2.8-2.84 may need careful equivalency explanation, strong SOP, strong project portfolio,
    English score, and possibly multiple applications.

Action before applying:
  - Verify Turkish GPA conversion with Monash admissions or an authorized agent.
  - Verify Electrical Engineering course structure/electives and any February 2027 details.
```

2. University of Melbourne

```text
Target program:
  Master of Electrical Engineering
  Course code: MC-ELECENG

Preferred specialisation/elective direction:
  - Low-Carbon Power Systems
  - Communications and Networks
  - Autonomous Systems if it supports embedded/control/edge systems

Why it fits:
  - Strongest prestige option in Melbourne.
  - Directly combines power grids, telecommunications, future electronics, and autonomous devices.
  - Good match for computer + renewable with digital communication support.
  - Industry projects/internships make the portfolio story stronger.

How to present our portfolio:
  - Tell the story as "embedded/FPGA renewable energy monitoring and control".
  - Mention prior ADAS prototype as computer vision and embedded systems evidence.
  - Use digital communication as telemetry/signal-processing support, not as an RF-heavy identity.

Risk:
  - Likely expensive and academically intense.
  - May be competitive.
  - Need to confirm admission equivalency and credit/advanced standing.

Action before applying:
  - Ask whether your current undergraduate background can reduce the duration.
  - Check exact fees, scholarship chances, entry equivalency, and English score requirement.
```

Tier 3: Technical stretch / outside Melbourne
---------------------------------------------

5. UNSW Sydney

```text
Target program:
  Master of Engineering Science, Electrical Engineering
  Program code: 8338

Why it fits:
  - Very strong technical option for renewable energy, power systems, and signal processing.
  - Good if you want a more research/advanced technical direction.
  - Strong support for digital communication and renewable/power depth.

How to present our portfolio:
  - solar-tracker-bess proves renewable monitoring/control interest.
  - fpga-digital-communications proves signal-processing and communication fundamentals.

Risk:
  - Sydney, not Melbourne, so weaker fit with the EMMS/Melbourne network plan.

  - More advanced/research-oriented; may be less direct as a professional conversion path.
```

Programs to avoid as primary targets for now
--------------------------------------------

Do not make these the main target unless the plan changes:

```text
1. Pure networking or cybersecurity master's
   Reason:
     Networking is only basic support for this plan.

2. Pure telecommunications/RF master's
   Reason:
     Digital communication is strong support, but RF/electromagnetics should not become the main identity.

3. Pure energy policy/business program
   Reason:
     It may weaken the computer engineering + renewable technical story.

4. Analog/RF/electronics-heavy programs
   Reason:
     Your strongest proof is software/FPGA/CV/renewable monitoring, not analog or electromagnetics.
```

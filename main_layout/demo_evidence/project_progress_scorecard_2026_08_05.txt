Biggest Mistakes To Avoid
-------------------------

```text
1. Do not present the three projects as unrelated.
   Present them as renewable source, digital communication, and network
   monitoring layers of one system.

2. Do not overclaim real solar production indoors.
   Say indoor power is low and the system is ready for stronger light/sun tests.

3. Do not overstate the mechanics.
   The pan/tilt actuator and prototype panel mount work, but the physical base
   and final demo mounting should still be presented as prototype-grade.

4. Do not call the proje1 FPGA proof a full wireless modem.
   Call it CRC/QPSK/OFDM packet profiling and FPGA-side communication pipeline
   validation.

5. Do not hide limitations.
   Clear limitations make the project look more mature.
```

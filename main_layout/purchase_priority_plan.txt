Purchase Priority Plan
======================

Goal:

Avoid buying expensive FPGA/RF/power hardware too early. Buy parts in an order that supports safe testing and keeps all three projects moving.

Buy first:

```text
1. Jumper wires and basic wiring kit
2. Logic level converter
3. Breadboard, if in stock
4. Basic resistor set
5. Ethernet cables
6. Multimeter if not already available
```

Buy soon after the basic build docs/code start:

```text
1. MCP3208 ADC or similar SPI ADC for solar sensing
   Current decision: buy now for proje3 real sensing stage 01.
2. INA226 or INA219 current/power sensor for solar sensing
   Current decision: buy INA226 now as calibration/reference and later I2C path.
3. Fuses and fuse holders for solar safety
4. Managed switch with port mirroring for network work
5. Small resistive loads
```

Buy after first simulations and basic HDL are useful:

```text
1. Low-voltage DC supply or small solar source
2. Proper gate driver
3. Buck converter power-stage components
4. ADC/DAC front-end for digital communication
5. FPGA-specific Pmod/FMC expansion boards
```

Procurement route note:

```text
If Turkish Digilent distributors are out of stock:
  1. Try direct Digilent-to-Turkey first.
  2. Expect extra end-use/export information for Turkey.
  3. If direct shipping is blocked, too expensive, or customs handling is too painful, use Germany/EU fallback.
  4. For Germany fallback, buy from Digilent or a reliable EU/Germany seller, then have one board brought to Turkey as personal study hardware.
  5. Keep invoice and original packaging.
  6. Recheck Turkey customs rules before travel.
```

Do not buy yet:

```text
1. High-power inverter
2. Grid-tied solar equipment
3. Expensive SDR
4. Large battery pack
5. High-voltage power electronics
6. SFP modules or DAC cable
```

Reason:

The first useful demos can be built with simulations, low-voltage sensing, dashboards, and simple FPGA/Orin communication. Expensive hardware should come after the architecture proves itself.

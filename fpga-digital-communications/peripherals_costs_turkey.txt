Still to check before final hardware:

```text
1. DAC module or fast external DAC.
2. Optional SDR board if RF OFDM is added.
3. Oscilloscope or logic analyzer.
4. SMA cables and adapters if RF extension is used.
```

Budget direction:

```text
Base digital-loopback MVP: low cost, mostly FPGA + Orin.
Analog loopback MVP: add ADC/DAC and wiring.
RF extension: defer until the digital baseband chain works.
```

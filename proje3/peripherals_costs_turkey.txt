Still to check before final hardware:

```text
1. 20W-50W 12V solar panel.
2. Gate driver IC/module suitable for the selected MOSFET.
3. Inductor, diode/synchronous MOSFET, capacitors, and shunt resistor for buck converter.
4. Load resistor bank or electronic load.
5. Bench supply with current limiting.
6. TVS diode and reverse-polarity protection.
7. Proper terminal blocks and enclosure.
```

Budget direction:

```text
Sensor + wiring prototype: about 500 TL to 1000 TL excluding FPGA and Orin.
Low-power PWM test: add MOSFET module, fuse, passives, and safe load.
Real MPPT converter: final cost depends on solar panel, load, gate driver, and power stage quality.
```

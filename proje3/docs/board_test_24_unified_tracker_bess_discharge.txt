Proje3 Board Test 24: Unified Tracker and Real BESS Discharge
==============================================================

Date
----

2026-08-11

Purpose
-------

Validate the complete live path with the four-LDR tracker, both PCA9685 servo
axes, panel-voltage telemetry, real INA226 battery discharge telemetry, the
proje1 CRC/QPSK/OFDM bridge, and the proje2 dashboard operating together.

Test conditions
---------------

```text
Nexys Video programmed and streaming MCP3208 LDR/panel samples over UART
Four fixed KY-018 sensors beside the independently moving panel
PCA9685 on Orin I2C bus 7, address 0x40
Pan servo channel 15; tilt servo channel 12
INA226 on Orin I2C bus 7, address 0x44, R100 shunt
INA226 bus-voltage calibration scale: 0.9501
Battery metadata: 2.5 Ah, 3.7 V nominal, 9.25 Wh
L9110 fan connected as the battery discharge load
MT3608 and TP4056 USB-C input disconnected
```

Unified Orin result
-------------------

```text
Packets: 60
Panel voltage: 49 to 210 mV
Panel estimated power: approximately 0.002 to 0.044 mW
Battery voltage: 2974 to 2990 mV
Battery current: -222 to -217 mA
Battery power: -659 to -647 mW
Battery state: discharging
Pan range: 90.000 to 28.294 degrees
Tilt range: 90.000 to 27.432 degrees
Tracker states observed: track-pan, track-tilt, locked
Locked samples: 7
Servo output: hardware enabled
```

Communication and dashboard result
----------------------------------

```text
Proje1 frames: 60/60 CRC valid
Proje1 sequence gaps: 0
Proje2 dashboard packets: 60/60 valid
Proje2 sequence gaps: 0
Frame profile: 161 bytes, 1288 bits, 644 QPSK symbols, 14 OFDM symbols
QPSK padding: 28 symbols

Compact frame example:
mix,seq=00059,pan=028.3,tilt=030.4,sun_pan=010.0,sun_tilt=037.9,err=019.8,st=seek,pv_mv=00187,pp_mw=00000,bv_mv=02974,bi_ma=-00222,bp_mw=-00659,bs=dis,act=hw
```

The dashboard kept panel power and real battery power as separate fields. The
small panel-power value is rounded to zero milliwatts in the compact integer
frame, while the full-resolution value remains in the dashboard and Orin CSV.

Evidence
--------

```text
proje1/data/unified_tracker_bess_comm_bridge_valid.csv
proje2/data/unified_tracker_bess_dashboard_valid.csv
proje3/data/unified_tracker_bess.csv (on the Orin)
```

Result
------

LIVE PASS for simultaneous two-axis LDR tracking, panel telemetry, real signed
BESS discharge telemetry, CRC/QPSK/OFDM framing, zero-gap UDP forwarding, and
dashboard logging.

Safety boundary
---------------

```text
The TP4056 USB-C input remains disconnected while the fan load is connected.
Temporary mechanical connections are used only for supervised bench tests.
The battery is removed and the servo supply is turned off after testing.
The Orin and Nexys remain on their normal external supplies.
```

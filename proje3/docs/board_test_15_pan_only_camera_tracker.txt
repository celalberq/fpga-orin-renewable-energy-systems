Proje3 Board Test 15: Pan-Only Camera Tracker Recovery Mode
===========================================================

Status
------

```text
READY FOR LIVE TEST
```

Context
-------

The tilt mechanism failed after the earlier two-axis live pass. The pan servo
and its PCA9685 channel 15 remain working. This test keeps the USB tuning
camera, INA226, UDP bridge, dashboard, and physical pan tracking active while
channel 12 is electrically and logically disabled.

Safety State
------------

```text
Pan servo:  PCA9685 channel 15, enabled, safe range 70-110 deg
Tilt servo: PCA9685 channel 12, broken mechanism, PWM released
PCA9685:    Orin I2C bus 7, address 0x40
INA226:     Orin I2C bus 7, address 0x44
Servo rail: external 5V 2A adapter with common ground
```

Before applying servo power:

```text
1. Disconnect the broken tilt servo connector from channel 12 when practical.
2. Fix the panel tilt mechanically near center so it cannot fall or bind.
3. Leave enough slack in panel and camera wires for the full pan range.
4. Confirm the pan base cannot slide or pull its cable.
```

Stage 3: Pan-Only USB Camera Tracking
-------------------------------------

The current PC address is 192.168.1.219. Recheck it if the network changes.

Run on Orin:

```bash
cd /home/msi
python3 proje3/code/orin_app/camera_light_tracker_udp_gateway.py --camera usb --camera-id 1 --camera-mount fixed --width 640 --height 480 --source-label "Orin USB fixed-camera pan tracker" --udp-host 192.168.1.219 --udp-port 5013 --max-packets 120 --interval 0.25 --threshold-min 80 --threshold-percentile 98.0 --min-area-px 3 --preview --drive-servos --pan-only --pca-bus 7 --pca-addr 0x40 --pan-channel 15 --tilt-channel 12 --pan-min 70 --pan-max 110 --pan-start 90 --tilt-start 90 --max-pan-step 2
```

`--pan-only` holds tilt at its start angle in the controller and releases
PCA9685 channel 12 before the tracking loop starts.

Pass Criteria
-------------

```text
PCA9685 startup prints: channel=12 tilt PWM released
Servo command lines mention channel=15 and never command channel=12 angles.
The pan axis follows horizontal light movement without buzzing or impacts.
The dashboard shows Actuator = servo (pan only).
Vision detection and confidence update normally.
Digital Comm Layer = CRC/QPSK/OFDM.
Invalid packets = 0 and sequence gaps = 0 during the active run.
```

Stop immediately and disconnect the 5V servo adapter if the pan bracket binds,
the servo buzzes hard, or any wire becomes taut.

CSI Camera Later
----------------

The USB camera on `/dev/video1` is the established tuning camera. When it stays
on a stand, use `--camera-mount fixed`; the pixel offset is mapped to an
absolute servo target around `--pan-start`. Use `--camera-mount moving` only
when the camera is rigidly attached to the pan platform. Return to
`--camera csi --camera-id 0` for the final onboard-camera demo after the pan
loop and replacement tilt mechanism are stable.

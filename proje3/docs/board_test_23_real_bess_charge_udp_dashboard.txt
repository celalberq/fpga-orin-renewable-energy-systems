Proje3 Board Test 23: Real BESS Charge UDP Dashboard
===================================================

Date
----

2026-08-11

Purpose
-------

Validate positive charging current through the real 1S battery path, then carry
the signed BESS telemetry through the Orin UDP gateway, proje1 CRC/QPSK/OFDM
bridge, and proje2 dashboard.

Test conditions
---------------

```text
Fan disconnected
MT3608 disconnected
Battery installed in holder
TP4056 powered from a working 5 V USB-A to USB-C source
TP4056 red charging LED illuminated
INA226 on Orin I2C bus 7, address 0x44, R100 shunt
INA226 bus-voltage calibration scale: 0.9501
Battery metadata: 2.5 Ah, 3.7 V nominal, 9.25 Wh
```

The charge-current direction is:

```text
TP4056 charger/B+ -> INA226 VIN+ -> R100 shunt -> INA226 VIN- -> battery positive
```

Positive INA226 current therefore means charging. This is the opposite sign of
the validated direct-fan discharge run.

Safety boundary
---------------

```text
The fan remains disconnected while charging because the TP4056 board has no
```

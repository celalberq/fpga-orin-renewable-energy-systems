Replacement Options
-------------------

Option A: fast repair, same micro-servo ecosystem

```text
Buy:
  - 1x MG90S 180-degree micro servo, or
  - 1x SG90/MG90S pan-tilt bracket if the bracket/horn broke

Why:
  Cheapest and fastest repair.
  Fits the current small pan-tilt geometry.
  Existing PCA9685 wiring and 5V 2A servo adapter can likely remain.

Risk:
  MG90S is still only about 2 kg-cm class.
  The same tilt overload can happen again if the panel is off-balance, wires
  pull on the hinge, or the bracket hits a stop.
```

Option B: recommended final-demo repair, standard-size metal mechanism

```text
Buy:
  - 2x MG996R 180-degree standard servos, not 360-degree continuous servos.
  - 1x metal 2-DOF pan/tilt bracket for standard 40 x 20 x 36 mm servos.
  - 5V-6V external servo supply rated about 5A or better.

Why:
  MG996R gives roughly five times the torque of MG90S.
  A standard-size aluminum/bearing bracket is mechanically more credible for a
  visible mounted-panel demo.
  It turns the current prototype-grade mount into a more serious final-video
  actuator.

Risk:
  The old SG90/MG90S plastic bracket does not fit standard MG996R servos.
  Current 5V 2A adapter is undersized for two high-torque servos under load.
  The heavier servo assembly needs a stiffer base.
```

Option C: overkill / only if MG996R still struggles

```text
Buy:
  - DS3218 / 20 kg-cm class 270-degree digital servo(s)
  - compatible standard servo brackets and metal horns
  - stronger 6V supply/BEC

Why:
  Very high torque and usually stronger gears/bearings.

Risk:
  More current, more weight, more tuning work, possible import delay, and no
  clear need for this small solar-panel prototype unless MG996R fails.
```

Current Recommendation
----------------------

```text
Updated middle-ground decision after price and compatibility review:
  1. Keep the existing 5V 2A external servo supply and PCA9685 wiring.
  2. Use 2x MG90S 180-degree position servos, not continuous-rotation models.
  3. Buy an assembled 3D-printed pan/tilt mechanism only if the seller prints
     it around the actual MG90S bodies and the horns supplied with them.
  4. Require centering at 90 degrees plus a no-binding movement test/video.
  5. Keep the panel light, balanced, and mechanically fixed independently of
     the servo horn.

Why this replaced the MG996R recommendation:
  MG996R is stronger but requires a larger bracket, stiffer base, and roughly
  a 5A supply. That cost and weight are excessive for the current small panel.
  MG90S is the best practical torque/size/power middle ground, while correct
  mechanical fit addresses the actual failure mode.

While waiting for the replacement:
  Continue with PCA9685 channel 15 pan-only camera tracking. Channel 12 must be
  disconnected or explicitly released and the physical tilt angle fixed.
```

Power and Wiring Notes
----------------------

Keep the existing logic wiring:

```text
Orin 3.3V -> PCA9685 VCC
Orin GND  -> PCA9685 GND
Orin SDA  -> PCA9685 SDA
Orin SCL  -> PCA9685 SCL
```

For high-torque servos:

```text
External 5V-6V supply + -> servo V+ rail or direct servo red wires
External 5V-6V supply - -> servo GND / PCA9685 GND / Orin GND common
Do not power high-torque servos from Orin pins.
Do not connect servo V+ to PCA9685 VCC.
```

Research Snapshot
-----------------

Useful facts checked online on 2026-08-07:

```text
MG90S:
  Typical micro-servo class, 4.8V-6V, about 1.8-2.2 kg-cm torque, about
  650-800 mA stall current.

MG996R:
  Standard-size servo, about 40.7 x 19.7 x 42.9 mm, about 55 g, about
  9.4 kgf-cm at 4.8V and 11 kgf-cm at 6V, about 2.5A stall current.

PCA9685:
  VCC is logic supply and should match the Orin logic side.
  V+ is separate servo power and should normally be 5V-6V for servos.

Local/nearby buying notes:
  Robotistan lists MG996R 180-degree around 209-223 TL depending on listing.
  Robotistan lists MG90S 180-degree around 115 TL.
  Robotistan/Motorobit list SG90/MG90S pan-tilt brackets around 48-58 TL.
  Motorobit lists a metal MG996R two-axis kit, but it appeared as notify/out of
  stock during this check.
```
